About Game

Galaxy Shooters is a high-action 2D space duel game where two starfighters battle across territorial airspace with laser cannons, energy shields, and tactical drops.

Intelligent Reactive AI

  1. Automatically tracks opponent vertical coordinates.
  2. Detects incoming enemy lasers and computes evasion trajectories to dodge danger.
  3. Scans for battlefield power-ups and moves to collect them.
  4. Automatically calculates optimal firing intervals.

Dynamic Health & Shield System

  1. Live animated health bars for both combatants.
  2. Temporary energy barrier absorbs incoming projectile damage.

Procedural Graphics & Shader Effects

  1. Multi-layered glowing laser surfaces rendered in real time.
  2. Directional muzzle flashes when blasters fire.
  3. Expanding shockwave and particle blast effects on projectile impact.

Full Audio System

Handling background soundtrack, laser fire, impact explosions, and victory jingles.
